## Strategy decision

**Context-aware rewrite.** Each em dash is replaced by judging its grammatical function:

| Function | Replacement |
|---|---|
| Aside / parenthetical (` — X — `) | Parentheses `(X)` or commas |
| Elaboration / definition | Colon `:` |
| Emphasis / range | Rewrite sentence OR semicolon |
| List separator | Comma or restructure |
| Date/number range | En dash `–` (NOT in scope but flag if found) |

Where rewriting produces awkward prose, the planner should rewrite the sentence rather than mechanical substitution. **The constraint is: no em dashes left in visible output, AND the prose must read at least as cleanly as before.**

## Variants

Only U+2014 (`—`, em dash) is targeted. En dash (U+2013, `–`) and double-hyphen (`--`) are NOT in scope. Confirm by Grep before completion.
