- Placed after the four About paragraphs, inside the same `Container`, page-scoped CSS in `about.astro`. Home stays lean — NO education block on Home (D-09); education reaches Home only via JSON-LD.

  - Group label: `.label-mono` `EDUCATION`, color `--ink`, followed by a 1px `--rule` divider (`margin-top: 16px; margin-bottom: 24px`, matching `.section-rule`).
  - Degree line (primary): `B.S. Computer Science` — `Western Governors University` — `May 2026`. Degree in `.body` or `.meta-mono` weight-500; institution + date in `.meta-mono` `--ink-muted`. Lead with the completed degree (D-10).
  - VT sub-note (light): `Transferred from Virginia Tech` — `.meta-mono`, `--ink-faint`. Honest: VT is where he started, WGU where he finished. Do NOT imply a VT credential (D-10).
  - Credential line: `LPI Linux Essentials` on its own line — `.meta-mono`, `--ink-muted`. Optional quiet `.label-mono` `CERTIFICATION` sub-label (frontend-design's call, D-09).
  - No GPA, no honors, no skill icons/progress bars (MASTER §8 forbids narrative graphics on About). Keep it minimal.
- Data: reads the new `src/data/education.ts` module (single source of truth, D-11). Phase 24 wires the SITE only; Phase 25 wires chat.

### Responsive behavior (375px + 1440px)

- **1440px (desktop, ≥1024px):** `.container` at `max-width: 1200px`, `48px` horizontal pad; `.section` margin-top `160px`; teaser is single-column full content width (no hero-style 12-col grid — the teaser is prose-flow like `.featured`). Summary + highlight capped at `68ch`.
- **375px (mobile, <768px):** `.container` pad `24px`; `.section` margin-top `72px`. Teaser stacks naturally (it is already single-column). Eyebrow `{role} · {dateRange}` may wrap to two lines — acceptable. Deep-link and its focus ring remain full-tap-height. Education block entries stack; the mono divider spans full content width. Verify no horizontal overflow of the long highlight string at 375px (wrap, do not truncate).
- No new breakpoints. Reuse the MASTER §4 tiers already in `global.css` (`≤1023px`, `≤767px`). Do not add media queries to `global.css`; any teaser/education responsive rule is page-scoped.

## Copywriting Contract

> **Voice: FIRST PERSON site-wide** ("I built", "I'm the solo contract engineer"). Only the chat widget uses third person (CHAT-06) and the chat pipeline is OUT of scope this phase (D-17). Every string below is first-person or voice-neutral fact.
>
> **Register: honest NEW-GRAD engineer with shipped production experience.** Never "senior" / "lead" / "5+ years". Never a self-diminishing "junior"/"student building side projects" label — the new-grad level is conveyed by content (recent grad, contract experience, seeking a full-time entry-level role), not a self-applied qualifier (D-07). Keep every claim accurate and non-inflated.

### ⚠️ EM-DASH LANDMINE (D-18) — mandatory manual verification

**ZERO em dashes (`—`) in all user-facing copy.** En dashes (`–`) are permitted (e.g. the reused `May 2026 – Present` dateRange). The project's automated voice / em-dash / banlist gates ONLY scan project & experience MDX — they do NOT scan `src/data/*.ts` (`about.ts`, `education.ts`), page-scoped teaser strings in `index.astro`, or `BaseLayout` metadata props. Phase 24 edits copy that lives entirely in these UNSCANNED files. Therefore every new About / education / teaser / SEO string MUST be verified em-dash-free BY HAND (grep for `—` / `—` across `src/data/about.ts`, `src/data/education.ts`, `src/pages/index.astro`, `src/pages/about.astro`, and any changed `description=` prop). Match the existing `about.ts` convention: curly quotes (`“ ” ’`) + non-breaking space (` `), no em dashes. This same manual check covers first-person voice and the no-"junior"/no-"senior" register in those files.
