This phase is a v1.2+ change that intentionally supersedes the three-link count for the nav going forward; that supersession is recorded here and in the phase SUMMARY, NOT by editing the locked spec. The genuinely-stale, safe-to-edit references are the in-code head comments (Header.astro ~line 6 "3-link mono nav"; MobileMenu.astro ~line 13 "Three stacked mono nav links"), which Tasks 1-2 refresh to the four-item order.

Responsive risk — the four-item nav could crowd at intermediate widths. This plan makes NO CSS change (render loop and `<style>` untouched). The intermediate-width behavior is verified visually at 768px AND 1024px at the 22-05 human-verify checkpoint (which now has explicit steps for both widths). If crowding is observed there, a follow-up CSS adjustment is raised at that point — the risk is surfaced here, not silently accepted.
